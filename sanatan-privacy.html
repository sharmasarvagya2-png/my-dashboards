Sanatan Sattva - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

The Sanatan Sattva YouTube app is a private tool used only by the owner of the Sanatan Sattva channel to upload Bhagavad Gita videos to that channel.

It accesses only the owner's own YouTube account, with the owner's consent, to upload videos and post comments on them. It does not collect, store, sell or share personal data from any other user, and it is not offered to the public.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
